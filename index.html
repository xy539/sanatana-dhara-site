About the Channel

What Sanatana Dhara stands for

Sanatana Dhara is dedicated to preserving and sharing the eternal wisdom embedded in the traditions of Bharatiya civilisation — from the Vedas and Upanishads to the epics, puranas, and living spiritual practices.

The channel explores philosophy, dharma, history, rituals, and the cultural continuum that has shaped one of the world's oldest living civilisations. Content is created with care for authenticity and depth.

Whether you are new to these traditions or a lifelong student, Sanatana Dhara offers a space to explore, reflect, and connect with the eternal flow.
